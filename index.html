How to get useful results

A few guardrails that make this toolkit more honest and useful.

Start with real information. Add actual topics, examples, metrics and audience details. Generic inputs produce generic outputs.

1. Scan. Enter at least two recent posts and your typical views. Look for promising signals, not guarantees.

2. Choose. Pick one topic with a useful story, strong audience response or unanswered question.

3. Repurpose. Add the real key points. The drafts are frameworks; review facts, platform rules and your own voice before publishing.

4. Measure. Return with results after publishing. Compare similar posts over time rather than treating one result as proof.

Privacy. This first version runs in your browser. Planner entries are saved locally on this device; the tool does not upload your metrics to a server.

Limitations. It does not connect to TikTok, YouTube or Facebook accounts, retrieve live trends, inspect videos, or predict platform algorithms. Those would require separate integrations or research.

Creator rule #1

Use your own baseline. A post with fewer views can still show stronger interaction from the audience that saw it.

What this tool won't do

It won't promise virality, fabricate analytics, or claim to know secret platform algorithms. It helps you make clearer decisions with the information you provide.
